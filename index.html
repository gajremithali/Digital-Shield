STEP 1 · CONSENT

Your data. Your control.

Before continuing, Digital Shield explains what this prototype is designed to access.

  • Only information and activity you explicitly authorize should be analyzed.
  • Passwords and private messages are not required for this experience.
  • Social-profile data will be obtained only through the platform's authorization where supported.
  • You can revoke permissions later.
  • The admin dashboard is intended for authorized project analytics.
STEP 2 · SELECT

Choose your digital platforms.

Select the social platforms you want to analyze. Digital Shield will request authorization from each supported platform before accessing permitted profile data.

No passwords or private messages are collected. The next step requests only the permissions supported by each selected platform.
STEP 3 · CONNECT

Authorize your selected platforms.

Digital Shield uses the platform's own authorization page. You choose what to allow; Digital Shield only receives the scopes the platform grants.

Some integrations require developer approval and credentials before live authorization can be enabled. Until then, the interface below remains a realistic preview and does not pretend that data was received.
