STEP 1 · CONSENT

Your data. Your control.

Before continuing, Digital Shield explains exactly what this scan is designed to access.

  • Only information and activity you explicitly authorize should be analyzed.
  • Passwords and private messages are not required for this experience.
  • Social-profile data will be obtained only through the platform's authorization where supported.
  • You can revoke permissions later.
  • The admin dashboard is intended for authorized project analytics.
STEP 2 · SELECT

Choose your digital platforms.

Select the platforms you want to analyze. Only platforms with a verified authorization flow can contribute data to your report.

Choose platforms1 selected
No passwords or private messages are collected. Only platforms with an available authorization flow can be connected; unavailable platforms are excluded from scoring.
STEP 3 · CONNECT

Authorize your selected platforms.

Digital Shield uses the platform's own authorization page. You choose what to allow; Digital Shield only receives the scopes the platform grants.

Only connected platforms are included in the analysis. If a platform is unavailable, Digital Shield will clearly leave it unscored rather than inventing data.
